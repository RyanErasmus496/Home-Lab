# Nextcloud — Self-Hosted Cloud Storage

## Why Nextcloud?

My family had a lot of old photos and documents stored on aging external hard drives. These drives were nearing the end of their lifespan and we needed a more reliable, long-term solution for storing our data — one that was both secure and easy to access for everyone in the family.

Rather than turning to a commercial cloud service like Google Drive or iCloud, I decided to self-host our own solution using Nextcloud. This gave us several key advantages:

| Factor | Commercial Cloud | Nextcloud (Self-Hosted) |
|--------|-----------------|------------------------|
| **Privacy** | Data stored on third-party servers | Data stays on our own hardware |
| **Cost** | Ongoing monthly subscription | One-time hardware cost |
| **Storage limit** | Capped by plan tier | Limited only by our own drives |
| **Control** | Provider controls your data | We own and control everything |

### Privacy

With commercial cloud providers, your data lives on someone else's servers — subject to their privacy policies, potential data breaches, and in some cases third-party data sharing. Self-hosting means our family photos and personal documents never leave hardware we own and control.

### Cost

Services like Google One or iCloud charge a recurring monthly or yearly fee that adds up over time, especially for larger storage tiers. Running Nextcloud on our existing home server means there are no ongoing subscription costs — just the electricity to keep the server running.

### Sentimental Value

Many of the files we are preserving are irreplaceable — old family photos, scanned documents, and memories spanning decades. Keeping these on failing external hard drives was a risk we were no longer comfortable with. Nextcloud gives us a reliable, redundant home for these files that we can access from anywhere.

---

## Why Not Just Use Google Drive / iCloud?

The short answer is **ownership and trust**. With a self-hosted solution:

- We are not dependent on a company's pricing decisions or policy changes
- Our data cannot be scanned, analysed, or monetised by a third party
- We are not locked into any single ecosystem
